Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Pocket ID makes ID and passport photos on your phone. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, does collect some data — see Advertising below.

Your photos stay on your phone

The camera is used only to take your photo. Removing the background, checking the photo, AI enhance (which only adjusts light and colour) and making the print sheet all happen on your device; your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. A photo is saved to your gallery (Pictures/Pocket ID) only when you tap Save, and you can delete it there at any time. Working copies are kept in the app's temporary storage and are deleted when you start a new photo or uninstall the app.

Data stored on your device

The app remembers your chosen photo type, background, language and units, and how many photos you have saved. This is saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We never receive a copy.

Permissions

Camera: to take your photo. Storage (only on Android 9 and older): to save the photo to your gallery. Choosing a photo from your gallery uses the system photo picker, so the app cannot see your other photos.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, such as a banner at the bottom of some screens, a full-screen ad when you open the app or after a photo is processed, and a short ad you can choose to watch before saving a photo or using AI enhance. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

What Google may collect

An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name); your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location; app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

Pocket ID has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your photos or your data anywhere. Your settings and working copies are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Working copies are deleted when you start a new photo. Your settings stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings; either one deletes them immediately. Photos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them there. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Children

This app is rated for users 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
